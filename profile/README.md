**Pragma Cell** helps small and medium-sized businesses through their digital transformation, in a **pragmatic and measured** way.
IT is not an end in itself: it is a means to reach your goals.

### What we do

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audit & strategy** | Assessment of your digital maturity, pain points, a prioritised 2–3 year roadmap and budget estimates. |
| **Implementation** | Custom development, integration of the tools you already use, data migration, testing and validation. |
| **Training & support** | Training for teams and managers, documentation, and support after go-live. |
| **Digital sovereignty** | Hosting your data in Europe, with independent providers such as Infomaniak. |

### How we work

- **Simple first** — automate what needs automating, nothing more.
- **Step by step** — quick wins first, then scale up.
- **Measurable** — every project starts from a concrete business goal.
- **Self-sufficient** — by the end, your team owns its tools.
